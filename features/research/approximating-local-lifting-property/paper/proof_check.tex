\begin{proof}[Proof sketch (superseded)]
The earlier draft identified $W_0^*\cong W^*/W_0^\perp$ and treated the
restriction $W^*\to W_0^*$ as a $p$-complete quotient map.  This is not justified
by Daws (2007); see Remark~3.1 in \texttt{p\_ALLP.tex}.
The recommended route is: (i) $\lambda$-$p$-LLP on $V$ implies approximate
$\lambda$-$p$-injectivity of $V^*$ (open for $p\neq 2$; known for $p=2$ via
Kye--Ruan and Dong); (ii) approximate $\lambda$-$p$-injectivity of $V^*$ implies
$\lambda$-$p$-injectivity of $V^*$ by the weak$^*$ compactness argument in
Theorem~3.8 of \texttt{p\_ALLP.tex}.
\end{proof}
